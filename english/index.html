01Role

What We Do

As the vehicle contact point and coordinator for the SHIIBA GROUP, we handle the following.

  • 01

    Vehicle pickups from leasing companies

    We act as the point of contact when vehicles are collected directly from leasing companies.

  • 02

    Auction-linked trade sales

    We handle coordination with auctions for trade (dealer-to-dealer) sales.

  • 03

    Coordination across the group

    We keep communication and coordination running smoothly between group companies.

  • 04

    Vehicle transport orders

    We also handle placing and receiving vehicle transport orders.
